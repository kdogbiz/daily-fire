DAILY FIRE

Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your phone.

Effective October 31, 2026

Daily Fire does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app stores

Everything you enter in Daily Fire — your goal, your check-ins, and your streak history — is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we don't do

We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Notifications

Daily Fire can send you a daily reminder notification. This reminder is scheduled on your device and only appears if you allow notifications. You can turn it off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
