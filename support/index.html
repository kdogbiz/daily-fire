How do I change my goal?

Tap your goal under the streak on the main screen.

Why did my streak reset?

Streaks count consecutive days. If a day passes without a check-in, the streak starts over. Your best streak is always saved in History.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Daily Fire.

Is my data backed up?

Your data stays on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your streak history.
